# 🚌 Smart Bus 360

### Intelligent Bus Management & Operations Platform

Smart Bus 360 is a modern and responsive **Bus Management System** designed to help transport organizations manage their buses, drivers, routes, trips, maintenance, safety, analytics, and operational activities from a centralized platform.

The project combines a professional public website with an enterprise-style operations dashboard.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of Smart Bus 360 are:

- Manage buses and fleet information
- Manage driver profiles and qualifications
- Track routes and trips
- Organize daily bus operations
- Monitor maintenance activities
- Manage inspections and work orders
- Track fuel and operational expenses
- Monitor safety and incidents
- Provide useful analytics and reports
- Improve operational visibility
- Provide a scalable and user-friendly transport management platform

## 💡 Why Smart Bus 360?

Smart Bus 360 brings multiple transport operations into one centralized platform.

Instead of managing fleet, drivers, routes, maintenance, safety, and analytics separately, the system provides a unified interface for managing and monitoring bus operations.
